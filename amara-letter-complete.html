My AMARA 🌌

Everything I Couldn't Say

My AMARA 🌌,

I wish I could sit beside you beneath a quiet sky and tell you all of this without having to choose the perfect words. Since I cannot, I’m leaving them here—gently, honestly, with no demand placed on your heart.

There are moments when I miss you in the smallest things: a thought I want to share, a song that suddenly feels familiar, a silence that seems to have your name in it. I don’t know if you understand how much you have meant to me, but I hope you never doubt that what I felt was real.

I’m sorry for the things I couldn’t express, for the words that arrived late, and for any hurt I may have caused. I can’t rewrite what happened. I can only be honest about what lives in me now.

If life has taken us onto different roads, I will respect your path. You do not owe me a reply, a promise, or a feeling in return. I only wanted you to know what I never managed to say face to face.

You will always be the sunshine in my life (you are my sunshine, my only sunshine, you make me happy you are my sunshine).

And here is my confession, simple and true: I love you, and I always will. Not as a claim on you, but as something my heart needed to say. Whatever the future holds, I wish you gentleness, safety, and a happiness that stays.

With love,
Adnan
